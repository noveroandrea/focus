Android — one tap

  1. Tap Turn on notifications below.
  2. Choose Allow when Chrome asks.

Nothing to install. Chrome delivers these even with this tab closed and the phone locked.

iPhone — add to the Home Screen first

  1. Tap the ↑ Share button, then Add to Home Screen. This is required — notifications do not work from a Safari tab.
  2. Tap the new Focus icon and allow notifications.

This page must be opened in Safari.

It is a few extra taps, once. After this the phone buzzes with the app closed and the screen locked, like any other notification.

iPhone — last step

You are in the installed app. Tap below and choose Allow.
