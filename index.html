Focus

Pair this phone so it buzzes when you drift off task.

Start on your computer

Open the Focus extension, go to Settings → Phone nudge and scan the QR code it shows. This page only works with the link from there.

Android — one tap

  1. Tap Turn on notifications below.
  2. Choose Allow when Chrome asks.

Nothing to install. Chrome delivers these even with this tab closed and the phone locked.

iPhone — add to the Home Screen first

  1. Tap the ↑ Share button in Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the new Focus icon from your Home Screen.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications there, then Allow.

iOS only allows notifications for web apps added to the Home Screen — there is no way around this step, and it only has to be done once.

iPhone — last step

You are in the installed app. Tap below and choose Allow.
